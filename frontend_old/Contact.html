How do I apply as an expert?

Head to our "Get Involved" section and select "Join as Expert." Fill out the application form with your background and expertise details.

Engagements vary from 3-6 months for specific initiatives to 12+ months for comprehensive transformation projects.

Compensation is based on expertise, experience, and engagement scope. We offer competitive rates and flexible engagement models.

Can my institution apply even if we're early in our transformation journey?

Absolutely! We work with institutions at any stage. Our framework is designed to meet you where you are and guide your growth.

What support do we provide during the partnership?

Regular check-ins, strategic guidance, expert coordination, outcome tracking, and continuous adaptation to your needs.

How quickly can we get started?

After initial inquiry, we typically schedule a consultation within 48 hours and can begin engagements within 2-4 weeks.

About Read Catalyst™

22+ years of industry expertise transformed into institutional transformation frameworks

Our Story

From industry observation to transformative action

For over 22 years, we've stood at the intersection of industry and academia. We've witnessed the transformation of hiring practices, the evolution of skill requirements, and the growing gap between what colleges teach and what industries demand.

This observation sparked a mission: what if we could bridge this gap systematically? Not through generic consulting, but through orchestrated expertise, structured methodologies, and outcomes-based accountability.

Read Catalyst™ was born from this vision — to transform engineering colleges into true ecosystems of excellence where students graduate not just educated, but industry-ready.
